Task-aware KG construction: 36-run hyperparameter sweep

Complete TSLA half of the balanced design — 36 configurations over a 200-day window, run as 13 chained jobs on EKS. Three findings: the evolved structural representation beats text-only in 28 of 36 head-to-heads and loses 8; path-chain features show no detectable effect on AUC; and no hyperparameter separates from the grand mean by more than two thirds of the between-run SD.

Paired t = +3.83 (SE 0.0141, n = 36). 28 of 36 runs beat their text baseline, by up to +0.265; 8 fell below it, by as much as −0.126. The gate is selective in the right direction — of 360 proposed additions, 66 were accepted (18.3%), mean Δ +0.029 against −0.119 for the 294 rejected.

Read the difference as a contrast between representations, not as a lift. The two arms share no features. build_day_feature_vector skips text entirely (feature_engineering.py:218, "using structural signals only"), so the evolved arm is path 384 + subgraph 96 + topology 8 = 488 structural dimensions with no text, while the baseline is mean-pooled article embeddings with no graph. No configuration in this sweep tested text + graph against text, so nothing here shows the knowledge graph adding to what the article text already carries. Answering that needs a third arm.

Correction (2026-08-09) — the gate does not protect the baseline. This section previously reported mean +0.020, min +0.000, and "21 of 36 runs ended exactly at baseline… no run ever finished worse." Baselines had been mispaired to runs; recomputed per run from MLflow parent/child links, the correct figures are above. Mean final AUC is unaffected at 0.617.

The mechanism: _should_accept_step (orchestrator.py:657) returns True whenever best_auc is None, which it is until a step is accepted — so step 1 is accepted unconditionally in all 36 runs, whatever its AUC. The gate's reference is the last accepted step, never the text baseline. "Evolution is strictly safe" is not supportable; "evolution wins 28 of 36 head-to-heads and loses 8" is.

C · Do path-chain features help?

No detectable effect. This is the sweep's central negative result and it is consistent across two independent tests.

path_mean_norm is the mean Euclidean length of each day's 384-dimension path vector, averaged over the run: 0 means the block is entirely zeros, ≈0.79 means most days carry real chain embeddings. It is preferred to nonzero_frac, which reads 1.0 as soon as a single day contributes, because embeddings are dense.

The second test is a group contrast. Path emptiness concentrates in the deep-hop arm — 7 of 12 h5 runs carry no path features at all, against 3 of 24 at h3+h4 — and that split is exactly balanced on the other three factors (lookback, steps and prompt in identical 1:2 proportions), giving an unplanned ablation:

t = +1.26 — not significant. The direction is nominally against path features, but at this sample size that is noise. The dose–response test is the stronger of the two, since it avoids the confound that h5 is path-free precisely because 5-hop chains are unreachable at this graph density.

F · Caveats

  • No text + graph arm. The two arms are disjoint representations (section B), so the sweep cannot say whether the graph adds to article text — only which of the two wins alone.
  • Short validation windows (~42 days) against 488 structural dimensions. AUC ranges 0.469–0.787 across configs differing only in lookback; the noise floor is high and the feature count exceeds the day count.
  • n = 9–12 per factor level. Adequate for large effects only; nothing here should be read as a precise effect size.
  • Stochastic evolution. Identical settings have produced path signal differing sixfold. Run-to-run variance may exceed factor effects.
  • The schema is advisory, not enforced. Every ontology inherits additional_node_types / additional_relationship_types / additional_patterns = true from create_base_ontology(), so the extractor may introduce types and patterns the schema never declares. Sweep graphs were ephemeral sidecars, so conformance was never measured on these runs.
  • Path-free is confounded with hop depth in the group contrast; the dose–response test is the one that avoids this.
  • Single ticker. All conclusions are TSLA-specific. MSFT is queued but unrun.

G · For discussion

  • Add a text + graph arm. Without it the sweep cannot answer whether the knowledge graph contributes anything beyond article text — which is the project's actual claim. This is a change to build_day_feature_vector, not to the design.
  • Should the schema be enforced? With additional_* left true, ontology evolution is a suggestion to the extractor rather than a constraint. Enforcing it would make evolution a real intervention — and would change what every result here means.
  • Step 1 is accepted unconditionally (section B). Gating it against the text baseline is a one-line change and would make "evolution never degrades the baseline" true by construction rather than false by accident. Re-run the sweep under that gate?
  • Path features cost real compute and contribute nothing measurable here. Change the feature construction, the downstream task, or the claim?
  • Is next-day direction the right target? It is near a coin flip, and may lack the signal to separate KG variants at this sample size.
  • Steps = 7 is hard to justify on this evidence — acceptance collapses after step 1 and the AUC gain over steps = 3 is +0.016.
  • Run MSFT as-is for replication, or fix the feature arm first?
