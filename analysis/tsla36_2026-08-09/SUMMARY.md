## Run selection

Six conditions, which together select exactly 36 runs:
experiment `0`, top-level, `FINISHED`, `time_window_days=200`, `ticker=TSLA`,
`candidates=2`, and `start_time >= 2026-08-08T12:00Z`.

The date cutoff is the one that matters: before `36f4f88`,
`embed_relationship_chains` had no remote branch, so chains were extracted and then
discarded at the embedding step and the path feature block was silently all zeros.
**Runs before that fix must not be pooled with these.**

`candidates=2` excludes the earlier `candidates=3` dispatches from the abandoned
design; `ticker=TSLA` because the MSFT half (chunks 14–26) is unrun.

## Two definitions that change the numbers

**"Final AUC" is the final *accepted* ontology**, reconstructed by walking step winners
and keeping the last one whose step was accepted. This is deliberately not the pipeline's
`best_auc` metric, which maxes over every candidate ever evaluated — including rejected
steps and the baseline — and so overstates what a run ended with. The two differ: for
`fe10350f` they coincide at 0.787, but in runs whose later steps were rejected they do not.

**`delta` is a contrast between disjoint representations, not an ablation.**
`build_day_feature_vector` skips text entirely ("using structural signals only"), so the
candidate arm is path 384 + subgraph 96 + topology 8 = 488 structural dimensions with no
text, and the baseline arm is mean-pooled article-text embeddings with no graph. Nothing
in this sweep tested text + graph against text.

## Findings that survived checking

- **Path features: no detectable effect.** h5 (0.635) vs h3+h4 (0.608), difference +0.028,
  SE 0.022, t = +1.26. Dose–response across all 36 is flat. 10 of 36 runs have an entirely
  empty path block — 7 at h5, 3 at h4.
- **No factor separates.** Widest spreads are lookback (0.597 → 0.643) and prompt
  (0.599 → 0.638), against a between-run SD of 0.065. Residual dominates.
- **Additions rank cleanly once the reference bias is removed.** Scoring a candidate as
  AUC-minus-current-best is unusable: the gate makes the reference the running maximum, so
  the score mostly reflects how lucky step 1 was in that run. Mean candidate AUC is flat
  across steps (0.52 → 0.51) while the reference climbs (0.563 → 0.626), pushing the share
  scoring "negative" from 62% to ~90%. Scored instead by **win rate** against the
  alternative proposed at the same step — same graph, same days, no reference bias —
  `RegulatoryBody`/`MENTIONS_REGULATORY_BODY` wins 78% and `EconomicIndicator`/`REFERENCES`
  wins 22% despite being the most-proposed addition of all. Grouped by
  **(node, relationship)**, since that pair is what a candidate proposes: 32 node types span
  80 distinct pairs.
- **Acceptance collapses after step 1**: 50%, then 6–15% from step 2 onward.

## One correction this analysis forced

The report previously claimed mean lift +0.020, minimum +0.000, and that 21 of 36 runs
"ended exactly at baseline… no run ever finished worse." Baselines had been mispaired to
runs. The corrected figures are above, and the mechanism is real:
`_should_accept_step` (`orchestrator.py:657`) returns `True` whenever `best_auc is None`,
which it is until a step is accepted — so **step 1 is accepted unconditionally in all 36
runs**, whatever its AUC. The gate's reference is the last accepted step, never the text
baseline, which is why 8 runs finish below it.
